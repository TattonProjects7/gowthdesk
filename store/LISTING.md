## Description

Shortlist is the investing app that lets you learn by doing. Practise with £10,000 of virtual money across UK and US shares, ETFs and crypto, using the same tools the big platforms give you, plus a short-finder no one else has.

YOUR PORTFOLIO, ON ONE SCREEN
• Total value with a chart you can scrub, day and all-time change
• General account, Stocks & Shares ISA and SIPP practice accounts
• Unrealised and realised P&L, dividends received and projected income

TRADE LIKE A PRO
• Market, limit and stop orders, fractional from £1
• Stop-loss, take-profit and trailing stops
• See the full cost before you deal, including FX on US shares
• Short of cash? Top up or move money without leaving the ticket

CHARTS AND RESEARCH
• 1W to 1Y charts with moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI and MACD
• Client sentiment, market calendar and results diary
• News and data-driven research notes on every instrument

NEVER MISS A MOVE
• Price alerts for above, below or a % move, as phone notifications
• Notifications when orders fill, stops trigger or dividends arrive
• Up to 50 watchlists

PUT IT ON AUTOPILOT
• Savings plans: weekly, fortnightly or monthly
• AutoInvest puts spare cash to work at your target weights
• Smart top-ups and one-tap rebalancing
• Five ready-made ETF portfolios from Cautious to Adventurous

SEE WHAT YOU REALLY OWN
• Look-through splits every ETF into its companies, sectors and regions
• Overlap check flags companies you hold through several funds

THE SHORT-FINDER
• Scans for stretched, rolling-over stocks and ranks the best shorts
• Squeeze-risk radar warns you off crowded trades
• Every idea pre-structured with defined risk and a reward-to-risk ratio

PRIVATE AND SECURE
• Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint app lock
• Sync across devices with an account, or use it without one

Shortlist is a research, education and paper-trading app. It does not execute trades, hold client money or give investment advice. Practice ISA and SIPP accounts have no tax effect. Prices are for information only.

## App Review notes — iOS

Shortlist is a paper-trading and education app. All accounts, orders and balances use virtual money; nothing is sent to a broker and no real funds are handled. You can test every feature without signing in: open Markets, tap any instrument, then Buy. Face ID is optional (Settings → App lock). Market prices are simulated unless the server has a data-provider key configured.

## Privacy

- Privacy policy URL: `https://<your-domain>/privacy`
- Data collected (only with an account): email address, user content (portfolio, watchlists, alerts), purchase history, device push token. None of it is used for tracking. Biometric checks happen on the device; nothing biometric is collected.
